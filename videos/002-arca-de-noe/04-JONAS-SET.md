# JONAS · ESCENARIOS PARA EL VIDEO DEL ARCA

Flujo: **1) imagen fija** con Jonas como ingrediente → **2) animar** esa imagen con el diálogo.

---

## A · EN TURQUÍA, FRENTE AL ARARAT (recomendado para este video)

### 1. Imagen
```
Same man from reference image: identical face, tousled light-brown hair, green eyes,
short stubble beard, olive linen button shirt open over an olive t-shirt.
He stands on a rocky overlook in eastern Turkey at golden hour, medium shot from the
waist up, body slightly angled, face toward camera, serious calm expression, direct eye contact.
Behind him, the snow-capped peak of Mount Ararat rises above a wide green-brown valley,
softly out of focus. Warm low sun from camera left, natural rim light on hair and shoulders,
light haze in the valley. Cinematic documentary look, photorealistic, natural skin texture,
50mm lens, shallow depth of field, 16:9. No text, no logos.
```

### 2. Animación con diálogo (gancho P01)
```
Animate the image. The man looks straight into the lens, chin slightly down, serious and
calm, and says in English with a deep, low, slow voice:
"It wasn't two of every animal. The Bible says seven."
After the line he holds eye contact in silence for one second.
Wind gently moves his hair and shirt, distant mountain stays still, natural slow blinking,
minimal head movement. Static camera with a very slow push-in. Ambient wind sound, no music.
```

## B · ESTUDIO TIPO PODCAST (para videos de otros temas o los Shorts)

### 1. Imagen
```
Same man from reference image: identical face, tousled light-brown hair, green eyes,
short stubble beard, olive linen button shirt open over an olive t-shirt.
Seated at a dark wooden podcast desk, medium close-up, leaning slightly forward on his
forearms, a professional broadcast microphone on a boom arm in front of him, off-center
so his face is fully visible. Serious calm expression, direct eye contact with camera.
Background: dark moody studio, acoustic panels, a shelf with old books and a small model
of a wooden ark, a framed vintage map of Mount Ararat on the wall, warm practical lamps,
subtle amber glow. Cinematic, photorealistic, 35mm lens, shallow depth of field, 16:9.
No text, no logos.
```

### 2. Animación con diálogo
```
Animate the image. The man leans slightly toward the microphone and looks into the lens,
serious and calm, and says in English with a deep, low, slow voice:
"It wasn't two of every animal. The Bible says seven."
He holds eye contact for one second after the line. Natural slow blinking, minimal head
movement, hands resting on the desk. Static camera, very slow push-in. Quiet room tone, no music.
```

## PARA LAS DEMÁS LÍNEAS (P02-P09)

Usa la **misma imagen del paso 1** como cuadro inicial y cambia solo el texto entre comillas y la acción.
Las líneas están en `02-GUION.md` (español) y `03-GUION-BILINGUE.md` (inglés).
**Límite de Flow:** ~8 s por clip = máximo **20-22 palabras en inglés** por toma. Las líneas más largas se dividen en 2-3 clips.
